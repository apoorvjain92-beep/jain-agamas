Uttaradhyayan 3 · Chaturangiya: The Four Rare Things

In one line: four things are hard to get, each harder than the one before: a human life, hearing the dharma, believing it, and actually living it. The one who gets all four ends suffering for good.

Source: Uttaradhyayan Sutra Part 1, chapter 3 (20 verses), translated pages 136–150 (data/translations/uttaradhyayan-1/). 10 minutes.

What the chapter teaches

  1. The soul has been everywhere. Driven by its own karma, a being is born sometimes among the gods, sometimes in hell; a king now, then an outcast, then an insect or an ant. It never tires of this wandering (v.3–6).
  2. Human birth comes rarely, only when the karma blocking it wears away little by little (v.7).
  3. Then, hearing the dharma is rare. Those who hear it take up austerity, forgiveness and non-violence (v.8).
  4. Then, faith is rarer still. Many hear the true path and still drift away from it (v.9).
  5. Rarest of all is effort. Many like the dharma but never put it into practice (v.10).
  6. The one who has all four sheds karma like dust. Purity stays only in a straight, honest heart, and such a soul blazes up to liberation “like a fire fed with ghee” (v.11–12).
  7. Even the detour is good. Some rise to the heavens first, are reborn as humans with every good thing (family, health, wisdom, wealth), wake up again, and finish the path (v.14–19).
  8. Know the four are rare, and act (v.20).

Key verses

1 · The four rare things

चत्तारि परमंगाणि, दुल्लहाणीह जंतुणो ।
माणुसत्तं सुई सद्धा, संजमम्मि य वीरियं ॥

For a living being in this world, four supreme things are hard to attain: human life, hearing the dharma, faith, and effort in self-restraint.

3 · Where the soul has been

एगया देवलोएसु, णरएसु वि एगया ।
एगया आसुरे काये, अहाकम्मेहिं गच्छइ ॥

According to its own actions, a being goes sometimes to the heavens, sometimes to hell, sometimes among the asuras.

9 · Faith is rarer

आहच्च सवणं लद्धुं, सद्धा परमदुल्लहा ।
सोच्चा णेयाउयं मग्गं, बहवे परिभंसइ ॥

Even when one happens to hear it, faith is extremely rare. Many hear the path that leads out, and still fall away.

12 · Like a fire fed with ghee

सोही उज्जुयभूयस्स, धम्मो सुद्धस्स चिट्ठइ ।
णिव्वाणं परमं जाइ, घय-सित्तिव्व पावए ॥

Purity belongs to the straightforward; dharma stays in the pure. Such a one reaches supreme nirvana, like a fire fed with ghee.

20 · Know it, and act

चउरंगं दुल्लहं णच्चा संजमं पडिवज्जिया ।
तवसा धुयकम्मंसे, सिद्धे हवइ सासए ॥

Knowing the four to be rare, taking up restraint and shaking off karma by austerity, one becomes an eternal siddha.

Verse 1 checked against the scan by hand; the others are from the machine translation and may have a small spelling slip. Scan of v.1

Song (English): Four Rare Things

Simple words for students (class 9). Bright, gentle folk-pop; warm young voice, acoustic guitar, light tabla. About 3 minutes.
▶ Listen

Intro
Mahavira said to his student one day:
“Four things are rare. Don’t throw them away.”

Verse 1: we have been everything
Life after life, we’ve come and gone,
Our own actions move us on.
Sometimes in heaven, sometimes in pain,
A king, a beggar, then born again.
We’ve been a tiny insect, an ant,
Still we want more, and stop? We can’t!

Chorus
Four rare things, count them with me:
One, a human life, like we have, you see.
Two, to hear what’s true. Three, to believe.
Four, to really do it. That’s the key.

Verse 2: one and two
A human life is hard to win,
It comes when lots of bad karma’s thin.
And even then, few get to hear
The good teachings, simple and clear:
Be patient, forgive, don’t hurt anyone,
Control yourself, and the work is begun.

Chorus

Verse 3: three and four
Some people hear, but don’t believe,
Some believe, then turn and leave.
Lots of people like what’s right,
But never try with all their might.
Hearing is good, believing is great,
But doing it is how you become great.

Bridge: an honest heart
Keep your heart honest, simple and clean,
That’s where goodness can be seen.
Like a fire when you add some ghee,
It burns bright and sets you free.

Verse 4: the goal
So now you know these four are rare,
Use them well, with love and care.
Control yourself, let bad karma go,
And become a free soul, forever so.

Final chorus
Four rare things, count them with me:
A human life, we’ve got it, see?
Now listen, believe, and really try,
And one day your soul will fly.

गीत (हिंदी): चार अनमोल चीज़ें

कक्षा 9 के लिए सरल भाषा। मधुर भजन-पॉप; युवा मीठी आवाज़, हारमोनियम, तबला, गिटार। लगभग 3 मिनट।
▶ सुनें

शुरुआत
भगवान महावीर ने कहा प्यार से,
चार चीज़ें मुश्किल से मिलती हैं संसार में।

अंतरा 1: हम सब कुछ बन चुके हैं
जन्म पे जन्म, हम आते-जाते,
अपने ही कर्म हमें घुमाते।
कभी स्वर्ग में, कभी दुख भारी,
कभी राजा बने, कभी भिखारी।
कभी कीड़ा, कभी चींटी बनकर,
फिर भी ना थके, इस चक्कर में फँसकर।

मुखड़ा
चार चीज़ें अनमोल हैं, गिन लो मेरे साथ,
पहला, मानव जीवन, जो है हमारे हाथ।
दूसरा, सच्ची बात सुनना, तीसरा, उस पर विश्वास,
चौथा, सच में करके दिखाना, यही है सबसे ख़ास।

अंतरा 2: पहला और दूसरा
मानव जीवन आसान नहीं,
बुरे कर्म घटें, तब मिलता यहीं।
फिर भी बहुत कम लोग सुनते,
अच्छी बातें जो गुरु चुनते:
धीरज रखो, माफ़ करो सबको,
किसी को दुख ना दो, संभालो खुद को।
(मुखड़ा)

अंतरा 3: तीसरा और चौथा
कुछ सुनते हैं, पर मानें नहीं,
कुछ मानें, फिर छोड़ें वहीं।
अच्छाई सबको अच्छी लगे,
पर करने में कोई ना आगे बढ़े।
सुनना अच्छा, मानना बढ़िया,
पर करना ही है सच्चा ज़रिया।
(मुखड़ा)

अंतरा 4: सच्चा दिल
सच्चा दिल हो, सीधा-सादा,
वहीं धर्म रहता, सबसे ज़्यादा।
जैसे आग में घी डालो तो,
तेज़ जले वो, वैसे आत्मा चमके।

अंतरा 5: मंज़िल
अब जानो, ये चारों अनमोल,
इनको ना खोना, समझो इनका मोल।
खुद को संभालो, बुरे कर्म छोड़ो,
आत्मा को हमेशा के लिए मुक्त कर लो।
(मुखड़ा)
